Challenges
Diverse PM responsibilities
• Developer (re)allocation
• Task management
• Customer contact
• Testing

Complex mix of contract and project models
• Waterfall - Agile
• Project - SLA
• Old - New customers
• Maintenance task - Project

Insufficient, dated, and redundant methods and tools for ISD management
• Different PM practices
• Less overview
• Redundant data entry

Intervention
1. Reformulate the ISD method to better reflect the practices and environment
• Manage projects based on customer relationships (Project, SLA, Maintenance tasks)
• Short descriptions based on checklists

2. Integrate ISD planning and control into one tool (JIRA)
• Manage development tasks, developers, and time
• Represent project management checklists as a JIRA workflow
