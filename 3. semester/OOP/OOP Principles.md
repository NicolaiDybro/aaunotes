### Polymorphism
Behavior depends on contex
Types of polymorphism
- Ad hoc: Works on a set of known types
	- Overloading: Overload methods
	- Coercion: Implicit type casting
- Universal: Works on any type
	- Inclusion: Works for all subtypes
	- Parametric: Generics
